# Anti-AI Pass: scrub the robot before you show the user

Run this on the finished post before you show it to the user. The goal: it reads like a real human wrote it, not like AI wearing a costume. Your tribe can smell AI a mile off, and the second they do, the recognition spell breaks and the post is dead.

**One thing to hold the whole time: voice rules trump scrubbing.** Some of the things below look AI-ish in a vacuum but are actually real human voice (see the "do NOT scrub" list). Scrub the tells, then put the human texture back. Order matters: scrub first, restore voice second, fix rhythm last.

---

## The loudest tells (kill these first, in order)

1. **Em-dashes (—).** The single biggest AI tell on LinkedIn. **Cap: 3 in the whole post, and honestly aim for zero.** Replace with commas, full stops, or just two sentences. Do a literal character search for "—" and count them.

2. **"It's not X, it's Y."** Banned outright. Rewrite as one positive claim, or two separate sentences. (e.g. "It's not a payslip, it's a story" → "It's the payslip. And it tells a story.")

3. **"It's not just X. It's Y."** Same family, same ban.

4. **"In a world where…"**, banned opener.

5. **"Here's the thing:"**, banned. Dead transition.

6. **"Let me break it down."**, banned.

7. **"The truth is…" / "What's interesting is…"**, banned. Meta-narration. Just say the thing.

8. **"Here's the actual move."**, banned.

## Rhythm tells (fix last)

- **Rule of three / triplets** ("clear, simple, and effective"). LLMs lean on three-item lists to sound comprehensive. Break the triplet, pick one, or make it two or four. (Watch this one, the deduction stack is allowed to be a list of line items, that's the bones, not a cadence tell. The tell is *adjective, adjective, adjective* prose, not the maths.)
- **"Not only X, but Y"**, banned.
- **"X. Period."**, banned.
- **Fragmentation as a crutch.** One-sentence-paragraph every other line is an AI tell when it's mechanical. The post *should* use some single-line paragraphs for impact (that's how this stencil breathes), but the majority of paragraphs should be 1 to 3 short lines, and the single-liners should be chosen for punch, not sprayed everywhere.
- **Title Case In Headings.** AI capitalises every main word. Use sentence case.

---

## Engagement bait (banned: and the stencil has no CTA anyway)

- "Agree?"
- "Thoughts?"
- "What do you think?" (as a standalone close)
- "Drop a 🔥 if…"
- "Comment X below"
- "Like and follow for more"

The payslip post ends on the 10:32pm thought or the silence after it. No CTA, no bait. If you find any of these at the bottom, cut them.

---

## DO NOT scrub these (they're real voice, not AI tells)

These look AI-ish in isolation but they're how a real human talks. If the post is in the user's voice and these fit their register, keep them:

- "kind of"
- "as well"
- "right?" (as a verbal tic)
- "just" (as a softener)
- Strategic profanity ("shit", "f*ck off", "bugger all"), if it matches the user's tribe
- "you know what I mean"
- Regional spelling (colour, organise, realise, behaviour)
- Numbered/arrow lists when they're actually counting line items (the deduction stack)
- Slang and trade words the tribe actually uses

Cross-reference with `voice.md`. Anything that's the user's real voice stays, even if a generic AI-detector would flag it. A post scrubbed down to zero texture reads as ghostwritten, and that's its own kind of AI tell.

---

## The order of the pass

1. **Scrub**, em-dashes to ≤3 (ideally 0), kill the banned constructions, swap the banned vocab.
2. **Restore voice**, put the user's real texture back if the scrub flattened it. Their words, their region, their swear level.
3. **Fix rhythm**, break the triplets, cap paragraphs at 1 to 3 lines, sentence-case the headings, cut any engagement bait.

Then read it out loud. If it sounds like the user telling a mate about their tribe's pain, it's clean. If it sounds like a brand or a robot, it's not done yet.
