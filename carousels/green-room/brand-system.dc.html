02 Background & Grain
BG-MAIN · full asset (portrait)
MIDDLE CROP · banners / posts / CTA (primary)
MIDDLE CROP + tilted content plate
BG-MAIN (bg-main.png) is the one and only background for all posts, carousels, banners, featured media and CTA images — the grainy emerald flow-field with soft sage highlights, grain already baked in (no extra noise overlay). Default usage is the middle crop: banners/CTA posts: light zoom (~140–160%) so sage glows show top and bottom; carousel slides: deeper zoom (~220–250%, like the Cover sample) with only a hint of glow at the edges. Stop slide: same BG-MAIN crop with a red radial glow layered on top (bottom edge, ~45% opacity) instead of the light green. Shift the crop window per slide for rhythm. Screenshots and code blocks still sit on the tilted low-opacity plate; the stop slide keeps its red-tinted gradient as the only exception.
