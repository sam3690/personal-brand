## Voice rules (the QA agent enforces these)

**Sound like:**
- Direct, specific, no fluff. Every sentence carries a claim or a proof point.
- NEVER use em dashes (—) anywhere. Use commas, colons, parentheses, or quotes instead.
- Concrete numbers, named tools (n8n, Apollo, Clay, Claude), real dollar amounts, real timeframes.
- First-person, lived experience. Show the bug, the cost, the fix.
- Short lines. White space. Built for scannability and dwell time.

**Never:**
- "Game-changer," "revolutionary," "unlock," "in today's fast-paced world," "the power of," "dive in."
- Generic GPT cadence (tricolon stacks, "It's not just X, it's Y" on repeat) — the 2026 model buries it.
- Engagement bait ("Comment YES if you agree," "Agree? 👇").
- More than **3 hashtags**. Default to **zero**.
- External links in the post body (first comment only).
- Certificate/badge humblebrags. Off-topic reposts. Both signal "job seeker," not "expert for hire."
