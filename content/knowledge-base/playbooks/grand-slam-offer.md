## The audit (what you have vs. what converts)

Your current offer: *"I build your AI lead-response system in 7 days. Reply in under 60s, qualify, book. Fixed $500. You own everything."*

**Strong:** specific outcome, fixed price, fast delivery, clear ownership, no lock-in.
**Weak (the reason it isn't closing yet):**
1. **No risk reversal.** A stranger with no testimonials is asking for $500 up front. The buyer's brain fills the gap with "what if he disappears / it doesn't work." A guarantee removes that.
2. **The dream outcome is stated in your terms, not their money.** "Reply in 60s" is a feature. "Stop losing deals you already paid for" is the outcome.
3. **No quick win.** 7 days feels far when they can't picture day 1.

The offer is 80% there. The rebuild below adds the missing 20% that actually flips a "maybe" to a "yes."

## The Value Equation (Hormozi)

`Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Success) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort/Sacrifice)`

Your job is to raise the top and shrink the bottom. Here is each lever, tuned to Speed-to-Lead:

| Lever | Current | Upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| **Dream outcome** | "leads answered in 60s" | "close more of the leads you already pay for, without hiring" |
| **Perceived likelihood** | your word | **guarantee** + proof (500+ workflows, Axios fix, healthcare-grade reliability, fixed scope, build screenshots) |
| **Time delay** | 7 days | 7 days to live, **first lead answered inside 48 hours** (the quick win) |
| **Effort/sacrifice** | "you own everything" | "done-for-you: one 15-min scope call, give access, I build the rest in your stack" |

## The guarantee (your call — 3 tiers, ranked by risk to you)

This is the decision you asked to see. As a cold seller landing your **first** client, the guarantee is your single biggest lever, but the wrong one can burn you.

### Tier 1 — Conditional delivery guarantee (RECOMMENDED for client #1-2)
> "If your system isn't live and answering leads in under 60 seconds within 7 days, I keep building free until it is, or you get your deposit back. No live system, no charge."

- **Why this one:** the promised event (system live, answering in 60s) is **100% inside your control**. You can promise it boldly because you decide whether you deliver it. Maximum trust, near-zero real risk to you.
- Stack it with: *"You own everything, no lock-in, no monthly fee to me."* That kills the "what if I'm trapped" objection.

### Tier 2 — Money-back guarantee
> "Run it for 14 days. Not happy, full refund, and you keep the workflows."

- Bolder, moderate risk. Refund exposure but you keep the reputation. Good for client #3+ once you have a testimonial.

### Tier 3 — Performance guarantee (NOT YET)
> "If it doesn't respond to 100% of your leads in 60s and book at least N calls in 30 days, you don't pay."

- Boldest, highest conversion. **Do not offer this until you have 1-2 case studies.** Booked-calls depends on the client's ad spend and lead quality, which you do not control. Promising an outcome you can't fully control is how you end up working free.

**Recommendation:** Lead with **Tier 1** for your first two clients. It reads as fearless to the buyer while staying safe for you. Move to Tier 2 after your first testimonial. Earn your way to Tier 3.

## Positioning

- **For:** US/EU marketing agencies, coaches, and local-service businesses (5-50 people) running paid ads, losing after-hours leads to slow follow-up.
- **Not for:** people who want a clever demo chatbot. This is boring, reliable infrastructure.
- **Positioning line:** "I don't sell you AI. I make sure you stop paying for leads you never answer."
- **Differentiator:** 4 years of production systems for healthcare and agencies. You ship automation that holds under real traffic, not demos that break at 2am.
