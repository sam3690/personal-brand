# First-Client Sprint — 1 Paying Client in 14 Days

> Your `revenue-roadmap.md` is a good 30-day plan across 4 businesses. This is the
> opposite: everything stripped down to ONE goal so you stop spreading thin. Until
> client #1 is closed, this doc overrides the roadmap's breadth. One offer, three
> lanes, daily minimums, one metric. Voice rule: no em dashes.

---

## The only goal

**Close 1 paying Speed-to-Lead client in 14 days.** Not audience. Not products. Not retainers. One cash client. Everything below serves that and nothing else.

Why the narrowing: you have ~26 lifetime cold touches and 1-2 LinkedIn accepts. That's not a strategy failure, it's a volume failure. A first client comes from concentrated volume behind one sharp offer, not from more plans.

---

## The one offer

The Grand Slam version from `grand-slam-offer.md`, with the **Tier 1 guarantee** ("not live in 7 days, you don't pay"). Do not vary it, discount it, or add options this sprint. One offer, repeated, is how you learn what's actually breaking.

---

## The three lanes (daily minimum effective dose)

| Lane | Daily minimum | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm reconnect** | 5 sends (A-C-A) | highest reply rate, fastest first close. See `warm-outreach-reconnect.md`. |
| **Cold email** | 10 new + all due follow-ups | volume engine. Routine handles automated sends; you keep the list full. |
| **LinkedIn** | work the accepts, not new requests | you're near the request cap. When someone accepts, fire the DM opener same day. |

That's ~15-20 fresh touches/day. Over 14 days: ~200+ touches. At your own funnel math (3-6% reply, ~25% of conversations become calls, ~30% of calls close) that is 6-12 conversations, 3-5 calls, 1-2 closes. The math works only if the daily minimum is non-negotiable.

---

## The 14-day shape

**Days 1-2 (setup, ~3h):**
- Pick guarantee tier (Tier 1). Bake it into `outreach-scripts.md` all 3 touches.
- Refill Apollo to 100+ live prospects (list is starving the funnel).
- Surface 10 warm names, draft + send first 5 reconnects.
- Record ONE 2-min Loom of a lead-response flow running (your single best sales asset. No Loom = no proof).

**Days 3-10 (grind, ~1.5h/day):**
- Hit the daily minimum every day. No zero days.
- Book discovery calls the moment anyone shows interest. Same-day if possible.
- Run the call outline in `outreach-scripts.md`. Send the Payoneer link on the call.

**Days 11-14 (close, ~1.5h/day):**
- Follow up every warm lead and every call that didn't close.
- "I'm taking 2 builds this month, 1 slot left" (true scarcity, you cap at 2).
- Close 1. Take 50% upfront. Start the build.

---

## The one metric

**Conversations started per day.** Not sends, not opens. A reply-that-continues. If conversations = 0 for two days straight, the problem is the offer or the message, not the volume. Fix the message, don't just send more.

## Do-not-touch list (this sprint)
- No new content pillars, no product launches, no retainer selling, no website rebuild.
- No new tools. No "let me first learn X." The stack is built. Use it.
- No dropping the price below $500 or the guarantee. Desperation discounts signal desperation.

## Kill criteria (honesty check)
If after 14 days of hitting the daily minimum you have zero calls booked, the bottleneck is the offer/targeting, not effort. Then, and only then, rework the ICP or offer. Not before. Effort first, diagnosis second.
