## 1. UF Designs — interior design studio (solo-preneur)
- **Problem:** manual consultation booking. Leads from Meta ads slipping while he ran the business.
- **Built:** full Meta (Facebook/Instagram ads) → WhatsApp consultation booking system.
- **Outcome:** WhatsApp response time went from ~24 hours to seconds. Booked 27 consultation meetings this month through the system, up from 7-8/month before, roughly 3.5x. His words: "big difference, saving me so much time."
- **Why it matters:** this IS the Speed-to-Lead offer, already shipped and running. Your single strongest proof point for the exact thing you're selling.
- **Use it for:** local/service businesses, solo-preneurs, anyone running Meta ads with manual follow-up.

## 2. Descent Perfumes — fragrance e-commerce
- **Problem:** manual social media + content posting, inconsistent customer generation.
- **Built:** social media marketing + content-posting workflows and AI agents.
- **Outcome:** WhatsApp assistant replies to customers instantly, content + ad pipeline is driving more post engagement, sales have increased, especially on new launches.
- **Use it for:** e-commerce / DTC founders, and the content-automation angle.

## 3. Medical-sales GTM manager — voice calling agent
- **Problem:** inbound client-acquisition enquiries + meeting booking eating his time and getting missed.
- **Built:** AI voice calling agent that handles enquiries and books meetings.
- **Outcome:** response time went from hours (or a full day) to seconds. Handles FAQs and collects lead info automatically. Closed 14 new customers in the last 3 weeks through leads it captured.
- **Why it matters:** closest analog to Speed-to-Lead in voice. Proof you handle real client-acquisition workflows, not toys.
- **Use it for:** sales/GTM leaders, B2B founders, anyone whose enquiries outrun their capacity to answer.
