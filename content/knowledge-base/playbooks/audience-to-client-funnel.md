# Audience-to-Client Funnel

> How build-in-public content actually turns into paying clients, so LinkedIn/X work
> isn't disconnected from revenue. Ties `../brand-profile.md` pillars to
> `first-client-sprint.md` and `proof-assets.md`. Voice rule: no em dashes.

## The chain

1. **Content (P1-P4 pillars)** earns a profile visit. The build-in-public pillar (P1, ~40% of posts) is the one most likely to read as "this person actually ships," which is the whole sale.
2. **Comments/DMs during the golden hour** are Engaged Leads (see `core-four-leadgen.md`). Reply to every one, the golden hour ritual doubles as pipeline, not just reach.
3. **Soft CTA** (follow, genuine operator question) turns a reader into a conversation. No booking-link CTA yet, per `../brand-profile.md`, the DM is the door.
4. **DM opens with curiosity, not a pitch** (same A-C-A instinct as `warm-outreach-reconnect.md`, applied to inbound instead of outbound).
5. **Discovery call** runs the CLOSER structure (`../frameworks/hormozi-closer-sales-script.md`), objections handled per `objection-handling.md`.
6. **Close** with the Tier-1 guarantee from `grand-slam-offer.md`.
7. **Delivered build becomes a new proof-assets entry** and a P1 post (anonymized if needed), closing the loop, next client sees a receipt, not just a claim.

## Why this matters now

Content is currently optimized for **audience growth** (`brand-profile.md`'s stated quarter goal), which is correct for a 9-week-old account, but growth alone doesn't pay bills. This funnel is the bridge: every inbound DM from a post should be worked exactly like a warm lead, not left on read. Track it as a 4th lane alongside the three in `first-client-sprint.md`, low effort (reactive, not a daily minimum), high value per lead (they already trust you enough to comment).

## The compounding effect

Each closed client, even a friend-favor build like the ones in `proof-assets.md`, is content. Each piece of content is a chance at a new inbound DM. Each inbound DM worked well is a chance at a client. This is the flywheel `../../business/revenue-roadmap.md` describes; this doc is just the explicit mechanics of turning the crank.
