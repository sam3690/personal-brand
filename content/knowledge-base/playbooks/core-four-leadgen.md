## The growth sequence: More, Better, New

Hormozi's rule: push **More** volume in a working cell until you hit a real constraint, then make it **Better** (optimize), only then expand to **New** cells or platforms. Skipping straight to "new" or "better" before you have volume is how effort gets wasted on the wrong fix.

**Where you are right now:** pre-More. Lifetime touches across all 3 active cells are low (~26 cold + a handful of warm + a growing but young content account). The `first-client-sprint.md` daily minimums exist to force More before anything else.

**Do not jump to Better yet:** don't A/B test subject lines or rewrite scripts repeatedly. You don't have enough volume for the data to mean anything.
**Do not jump to New yet:** no ad budget, no ad account, and paid ads on an unproven offer just buys expensive silence. Add this cell only after 2+ closed clients validate the message converts.

## Engaged Leads (the only leads that count)

Hormozi's definition: a lead is only "engaged" if they've taken a real action, replied, commented, clicked, booked. This is exactly the `first-client-sprint.md` "conversations started per day" metric. A send is not a lead. A reply is.
