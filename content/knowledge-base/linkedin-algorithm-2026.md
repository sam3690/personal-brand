## A. The 5 structural changes for 2026 (what the machine now does)

1. **360Brew model scores DWELL TIME, not clicks.** A 150B-parameter model measures how long
   people actually *read*. Deep, substantive posts get amplified; shallow posts get buried.
   → Write for read-time: a real idea, developed, worth stopping for.

2. **Profile–content alignment is enforced.** The algorithm cross-references every post against
   your headline, About, and experience. Aligned = broader reach + "higher confidence."
   Misaligned (e.g. an AI-automation expert posting Palestine reposts and salary stories) =
   **immediate reach suppression.** → 100% on-pillar, every post. This is Usama's #1 leak.

3. **AI-generated content is detected and suppressed.** Generic, repetitive, obviously-GPT text
   is flagged with high accuracy and doesn't leave your immediate network. Also penalized:
   engagement bait ("Comment YES if you agree"), recycled thought-leadership, bot comments.
   → Every post must carry specific, first-hand detail no model could invent.

4. **Hashtags barely matter now.** The algorithm reads *recurring themes in your content* to
   decide your topic — not hashtags. **More than 3–5 hashtags = spam signal.**
   → Max 3 hashtags, often zero. (Usama was using 20–25. Stop.)

5. **Creator Mode is gone** (features moved into profile settings). Audit settings; the
   "creator boost" now comes from consistency + on-topic history, not a toggle.

## B. The 4-phase scoring model (how a post lives or dies)

**Phase 1 — Quality Filter (0–5 sec).** Auto-scan for spam, dense/thin formatting, and
external links that cut dwell time.
- No external links in the body → put them in the **first comment**.
- Format for scannability: short lines, white space, clear structure.
- The **first line (hook)** is the only thing that passes the filter fast.

**Phase 2 — Seed Audience Test (first 60–90 min).** Shown to ~1–5% of your 1st-degree network.
Measures **engagement velocity**. *This is where Usama's posts die* — tiny + cold seed (271
followers). The first 50–100 reactions inside 60 min is the single biggest predictor of reach.
→ Golden-hour ritual (Section E) is mandatory, not optional.

**Phase 3 — Algorithmic Expansion.** If Phase 2 wins, reach expands to 2nd-degree + interest
audiences. Weighted here:
- **Comment quality > quantity** — "great!" ≈ worthless; a substantive reply triggers far more distribution.
- **Reply loops** — author replying to comments extends post lifespan.
- **Saves** — a private high-intent signal.

**Phase 4 — Editorial & Viral Layer (top ~5%).** Humans may surface it to LinkedIn News /
Collaborative Articles → lifespan jumps from 24–48h to 7+ days. Gets here: comment-to-like
ratio >30%, genuine debate / strong emotion, consistent posting history.

## C. The 5 engagement signals, ranked by weight

| Signal | Weight | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Comments** (substantive) | 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥 Highest | End every post with ONE specific question. A post with 50 comments beats one with 500 likes. |
| **Reposts** | 🔥🔥🔥🔥 Very High | Make a saveable/shareable point (framework, contrarian truth). |
| **Saves / bookmarks** | 🔥🔥🔥🔥 Very High | Lists, step-by-steps, frameworks people want to keep. |
| **Reactions (likes)** | 🔥🔥🔥 High | Volume matters, but *speed in hour 1* matters more. |
| **Profile clicks** | 🔥🔥 Medium | A hook that makes people wonder "who wrote this?" |
| **External link clicks** | ⚠️ NEGATIVE | LinkedIn penalizes off-platform traffic. Links → first comment, after 10–15 min. |

**Design law:** start conversations, don't collect reactions.

## D. Timing (post in the AUDIENCE's timezone — Usama's ICP skews US/EU founders)

**Tier 1 (highest reach):**
- Tuesday 7:30–9:00 AM — pre-meeting check
- Wednesday 8:00–10:00 AM — mid-week professional peak
- Thursday 7:30–9:00 AM — pre-Friday urgency

**Tier 2:** Mon 9–11 AM · Tue 12–1 PM (lunch/mobile) · Wed 5:00–6:30 PM

**Tier 3:** Fri before 9 AM (drops sharply after noon) · Sun 7–9 PM (week-ahead framing)

**Avoid:** Saturday · after 6 PM Friday · random "off" hours (no audience to engage).

## E. The Golden-Hour ritual (first 60–90 min — do every time)

1. Post at a Tier-1 window.
2. **Reply to every comment immediately** (especially first 2 hours) — reply loops = reach.
3. **Do NOT edit the post in the first hour** (LinkedIn penalizes early edits).
4. If using a link, drop it in the first comment **after 10–15 min**, not instantly.
5. **Engage with 3–5 other relevant posts right before and after publishing** to warm the feed.

## F. Content formats, ranked by algorithmic weight

1. 🏆 **Carousel / Document** — 48–72h shelf life (vs 24h). Each swipe = a dwell-time signal.
   Best for frameworks, step-by-steps, breakdowns. Slides must look clean, never template-blurry.
2. 🥈 **Native video** (uploaded, not a YouTube link) — auto-play raises dwell; **captions required**
   (80% watched on mute); keep under 90 sec.
3. 🥉 **Single image** — only if the image carries a *standalone* framework/infographic, not decoration.
4. **Text-only** — can go huge *if* the hook is irresistible; the hook does 100% of the work.
5. ⚠️ **Polls** — downweighted; once a month max.

**What kills reach:** links in body · blurry/template carousels · re-sharing news without original
commentary · >3–5 hashtags · off-topic content · obvious AI text · engagement bait.

## G. The 6 hook formulas (first 2 lines = the whole game; only ~2–3 lines show before "see more")

1. **Counterintuitive** — "Posting every day is making your LinkedIn worse, not better."
2. **Specific-number promise** — "I grew 0→40K in 14 months. Here's the exact system 👇"
3. **Personal confession** — "I made $0 from LinkedIn for 8 months. Then I changed one thing."
4. **Bold claim** — "The algorithm doesn't reward consistency. It rewards conversation."
5. **"If you" targeting** — "If you're a founder posting and getting silence — this is why."
6. **List tease** — "7 posts that each drove 10+ inbound conversations (and what worked):"

Hooks fail when they're vague, hedged, throat-clearing ("I've been thinking about..."), or give
the payoff away before "see more."

---

## H. Consistency (the compounding lever)

- **4 decent posts/week beats 1 perfect post/month.**
- Missing a week can cut reach **20–40%** temporarily.
- Daily presence builds algorithmic trust faster than sporadic long-form.
- Cadence for this system: **4 posts/week**, Tue/Wed/Thu Tier-1 + one Mon or Fri-AM.
