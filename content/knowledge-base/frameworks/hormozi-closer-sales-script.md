# Hormozi's CLOSER Framework — Sales Call Structure

> Source: Alex Hormozi, developed from 4,000+ personal closes and training 116+ salespeople
> across his portfolio. Feeds `../playbooks/objection-handling.md` and replaces the loose
> 5-step call outline in `../../business/outreach-scripts.md`. Voice rule: no em dashes.

## The six steps

- **C — Clarify why they're here.** Don't assume. Ask what made them take the call today.
- **L — Label their problem.** Reflect it back in their words, not yours. "So the issue is X" builds trust before you've sold anything.
- **O — Overview their past attempts (the pain cycle).** What have they tried, what didn't work, what did it cost them (time, money, missed deals). This is the diagnosis phase, most of the call should live here.
- **S — Sell the vacation, not the plane ticket.** Sell the outcome (leads answered, deals not lost) not the mechanism (n8n, WhatsApp API).
- **E — Explain away concerns.** Handle objections directly, see `../playbooks/objection-handling.md`.
- **R — Reinforce the decision.** After they say yes, confirm it was the right call. Buyer's remorse kills deals between the call and the deposit landing.

**The core correction:** most sellers spend 80% of the call on S (pitching) and 20% on C-L-O (diagnosis). Hormozi's data says flip it. The sale is mostly made before you ever describe the system.

## Applied to Usama's discovery call

Your current outline (`../../business/outreach-scripts.md`) is 5 steps: describe the pain, quantify it, describe the build, price+timeline, send payment link. That's mostly O and S, missing an explicit C (why today, why now), a real L (label it back to them before moving on), and R (confirming the yes after they say it).

**The upgraded call:**
1. **C:** "What made you want to take this call today?"
2. **L:** "So the real issue is [their words], not just 'leads are slow.'" Wait for agreement.
3. **O:** Full pain-cycle dig, what they've tried, what it's costing them monthly (ties to the quantify step you already do).
4. **S:** Describe the outcome (never miss another lead, stop losing deals to slow response) before the mechanism.
5. **E:** Run the relevant objection from `../playbooks/objection-handling.md`.
6. **R:** After they agree to the $500 + Payoneer link: "Good call, most people who put this off keep losing the exact leads we just talked about."

## Why this matters for a first-time seller
You have no reps yet. This structure means you don't have to improvise the hard parts, diagnosis and objection handling, live. Practice the six letters until they're reflexive; the framework carries you through the parts that would otherwise go blank.
