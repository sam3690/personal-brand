# AI Agency Niche Selection & Productization (2026)

> Source: 2026 AI-automation-agency market research (Agensi, Ciela AI, Moneylab, and others).
> Validates/extends `../../business/revenue-roadmap.md`'s ICP. Voice rule: no em dashes.

## Niche-selection criteria (the market consensus)
A good niche has all four:
1. Buyers have money.
2. The problem is repetitive and boring (not a one-off).
3. Current solutions are expensive or manual (no in-house AI expertise).
4. The outcome is describable in one sentence.

**2026's named good niches include:** construction estimating, small-firm legal intake, real-estate lead qualification, dental/medical patient reminders, e-commerce returns processing.

Keep the guarantee framing, it protects revenue better than a fully free pilot while giving the same risk reversal.

## Next niche move (later, not now)
Once the `first-client-sprint.md` kill-criteria checkpoint passes (i.e., you have 1-2 closes), consider narrowing further into ONE of: local-service (med spa/interior/roofing) or medical-adjacent. A tighter niche means proof-assets.md case studies compound faster (same-industry social proof hits harder) and cold email subject lines can go industry-specific instead of generic. Don't narrow before you have the data to know which niche is actually replying.
