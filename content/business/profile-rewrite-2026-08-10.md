Three builds, three numbers.

Reply time on inbound: about 24 hours, now seconds.
Consultations: 7 or 8 a month, now 27.

By the time anyone called back, they had booked someone else.

So I build the thing that answers instead. Every inquiry, whether it is a form, WhatsApp, an Instagram
DM or a missed call, gets a reply in under 60 seconds, gets asked the questions you would ask, and gets
booked into your calendar. It logs to your CRM so nothing goes missing. It answers first and you are
one step away when it matters. The comparison is not this versus your best person on the phone, it is
this versus the call nobody returned.

How I work:

- Live in 7 days or you do not pay.
- Fixed price, half up front. No retainer and no subscription of mine to keep paying.
- You own the build. Documentation written and handed over, and 30 days of fixes included.
- When it breaks, you message me. I am the point of contact, not a support queue.

Where I am: Pakistan, working US hours. I hold <<9am to 1pm ET>> every weekday as standard overlap and
I move for calls outside it. I am saying it here because you would find it out anyway, and because what
I sell is response time, so mine should be the first proof of it.

Recent builds, anonymized: a design studio running Meta and Instagram ads into a WhatsApp booking flow;

If you run a small shop, you probably already know which inquiry you dropped last week. Message me and
