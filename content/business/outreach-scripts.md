## Upwork proposal template
Hi, I can have this live in 7 days, fixed price.

What you'll get: [restate their ask in one line]. Built in n8n so you own it, no monthly fees to me unless you want ongoing management.

Relevant: I've built 500+ AI workflows, 4 years of production backend systems (healthcare-grade reliability), and I recently shipped a fix to Axios, the HTTP library most Node apps use. I build boring automation that doesn't break at 2am.

First step: 15-min call to lock scope. Then 50% to start, 50% on delivery.

## Discovery call outline (15 min)
1. "Walk me through what happens today when a lead comes in." (let them describe the pain)
2. Quantify: leads/month × close rate × deal size → what one saved deal is worth
3. Describe the build in their nouns (their form, their calendar, their CRM)
4. Price + timeline: "$500 fixed, live in 7 days, 50% to start. I only take 2 of these a month."
5. Send Payoneer link while still on the call.

- 10 new cold emails (Apollo list)
- 10 LinkedIn connection requests w/ no note (higher accept rate) OR DMs to accepted
- All due follow-ups (touch 2/3)
- Reply to every comment on your posts (golden hour doubles as pipeline)
