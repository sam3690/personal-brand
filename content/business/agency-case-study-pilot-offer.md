# Getting the first agency case study

> **Owner: Usama.** This is the highest-value open item on the whole board (`../strategy/tribe.md`
> §8.1, §10 open action 1). Every proof asset right now was delivered to a business owner, not an
> agency, so "I do this for agencies" is a positioning bet. One delivered agency client, named or
> anonymized, closes that gap permanently and unlocks the real thing all four Shape-A posts are
> pointing at. I cannot land this for you: it needs a real conversation with a real agency owner. What
> I can do is make that conversation as cheap as possible to start.

## The offer to make

**A free or steeply discounted Shape-A pilot on ONE client account, in exchange for case-study
rights.** Concretely:

- You install lead-response (the same build described in `../strategy/current-strategy.md` LANE 2
  OFFER) on **one of their client accounts**, not their own new-business inbox. Shape A is the bigger,
  more agency-specific proof, and it's the one this whole re-point is betting on.
- Price: **free, or a token $250-500** (not the full $1,500) explicitly framed as "I want one agency
  case study, this is a founding-partner rate, not a discount you'll see again." Free is fine if it
  gets you a yes faster; the case study is worth more than the fee right now.
- In writing, before you build anything: naming permission (or agreement to anonymize, decided up
  front, not renegotiated after), a testimonial with a number in it, and permission to screenshot the
  before/after (reply time, booked calls, whatever moved).
- Same 7-day live guarantee as every other build. Don't lower your own delivery bar because the price
  is low, that undercuts the exact case study you're trying to earn.

## Who to ask first

Warm before cold, in this order:
1. **Anyone already in your network who runs an agency.** The 2026-08-03 graph audit found the
   371-connection graph is thin on agency owners specifically (dense in automation builders instead),
   but "thin" isn't zero. Search your own connections for agency-owner titles before doing anything
   else, a warm ask closes faster than a cold one and skips gates 1-3 (`lakajev-linkedin-leadgen.md`
   §6.1).
2. **Anyone one referral away.** Ask 2-3 people you already know (clients, past coworkers, the
   interior-design or medical-sales contacts) if they know an agency owner who'd want this. A referred
   intro arrives pre-vouched.
3. **Cold, via the DM lane, once it's running.** `lakajev-linkedin-leadgen.md` §7.1 now targets agency
   owners directly. Once that lane is live (see item below), a pilot offer is a strong reason to open a
   conversation, not the opener itself: lead with the recognition question, offer the pilot only after
   they've engaged.

## Draft DM / message (adapt the bracketed part to the real person, never send it unedited)

> Hey [Name], saw [specific thing: their post about a client review, a churn story, a retainer they
> mentioned]. Quick question since you run an agency: when a lead from one of your client's campaigns
> sits for a day before anyone replies, does that ever come back to you?
>
> I build the fix for exactly that (production systems, not demos, 4 years doing this for healthcare
> orgs where a broken workflow is a much bigger problem than a missed lead). I'm looking for one agency
> to build this on for a single client account, at cost, in exchange for being able to show the result.
> No pitch if it's not useful to you, just tell me if this is a real problem on any of your accounts.
