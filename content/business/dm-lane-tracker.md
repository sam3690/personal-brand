## The two matched batches (spec section 5), 20 minutes, run once

Purpose: settle whether recipient seniority and company size move acceptance rate. No published study
answers this with disclosed methodology. 50 of Usama's own requests will.

**Both batches: blank requests, no note.** Free accounts get 5 notes a month, so the note debate is
moot. Send A and B in the same sitting so the day-7 read is like for like.

### How to find them without Sales Navigator

Free people search has no company-headcount filter, that one is Sales Navigator only. So headcount gets
checked by hand at qualify step 1 (open the company page, read the employee count). The search only has
to get the pool roughly right.

**Batch A pool.** People search, keywords `founder OR owner OR co-founder`, Locations = United States,
United Kingdom, Canada, Australia. Then scan headlines for: agency, studio, recruiting, consulting,
creative, media. Open the company page, keep only 2-20 employees. Apply the full qualify list in spec
section 3, especially the peer disqualifier (their product is software, marketing automation, data or
technical consulting = skip) and the GoHighLevel check.

**Batch B pool.** Same search, keyword `CEO`, same locations, same industries. Keep only 50-200
employees. Batch B intentionally ignores the qualify list, that is the point of the test.
