# Revenue Roadmap, 4 Businesses, 30-Day Plan
Created: 2026-07-03 · Constraints: cash in 30 days, 10–15 hrs/wk, full sales willingness, US/EU clients, payments sorted.

## The 4 businesses (in order of speed-to-cash)

### 1. Speed-to-Lead Install (productized n8n service), PRIMARY, weeks 1–4
**Offer:** "I build your AI lead-response system in 7 days. Every lead gets a reply in under 60 seconds (WhatsApp/email), gets qualified, and books into your calendar. Fixed price. You own everything."
**Price:** $500 for first 2 clients (speed + testimonial), then $750, then $900.
**Buyer:** US/EU marketing agencies, coaches, home-service/local-service businesses, 5–50 employees, running paid ads (they feel slow-lead pain hardest).
**Channel:** Apollo cold email (10/day) + LinkedIn DM (10/day) + Upwork.
**Why first:** matches your drafted case-study content (WhatsApp lead follow-up), pricing already validated in your angle bank, deliverable in ≤7 days.

### 2. AI SDR Retainer, upsell path, weeks 3+
**Offer:** "$50/mo AI SDR stack, built and managed. I run your outbound: list building, personalized cold email, reply classification, meeting booking. You pay for meetings, not software."
**Price:** $750/mo (first 2), then $1,000–$1,500/mo.
**Buyer:** same ICP; every Speed-to-Lead client is a warm upsell.
**Asset reuse:** `content/lead-magnets/stack-ai-sdr.md` IS the delivery blueprint.
**Why:** this is the MRR engine. 3 retainers = $2,250–4,500/mo recurring.

### 3. Digital Products (the product platform (Paddle or direct Payoneer checkout; Gumroad cannot pay out to Pakistan)), passive lane, week 2 onward
**Offer:** package existing lead magnets as paid products:
- "$50 AI SDR Stack, full n8n build + templates" → $49
- "Prompt-to-Pipeline Playbook" → $29
- "GTM Agent in Claude Code, complete walkthrough" → $79
Keep free versions as lead magnets (lite), sell the full build files + video walkthrough.
**Channel:** LinkedIn CTA posts, first comment link, the product platform (Paddle or direct Payoneer checkout; Gumroad cannot pay out to Pakistan) Discover, n8n community.
**Expectation:** $100–500/mo early. Real value = strangers paying you is proof, and buyers become service leads.

### 4. LinkedIn Content Engine Install, high-ticket, month 2+
**Offer:** "The exact AI content system I run: 6 managed agents, algorithm-QA'd posts, drafted to your scheduler weekly. Installed in your Claude Code in one week."
**Price:** $1,500 setup + optional $300/mo maintenance.
**Buyer:** founders/consultants who watched your build-in-public posts.
**Why later:** needs your own account's growth as the proof. Your follower chart IS the sales page. Don't sell until your numbers make the case.

## The honest math
- 400 outreach touches/mo → 3–6% reply → 12–24 conversations → 4–6 calls → 1–2 closes. That's the funnel; the variable you control is daily volume + follow-up discipline.
- Month 2–3 trajectory: 2–3 retainers ($2–4k MRR) + rising fixed-price installs + product trickle → $3–5k/mo by day 90 is the realistic good outcome.
- Every client build becomes a case-study post → inbound slowly replaces outbound. That's the flywheel.

## Rules
- Never start work without 50% upfront (Payoneer link).
- First 2 clients: underprice, overdeliver, extract testimonial + numbers.
- Outreach is the job. Content supports it. Products compound it. Don't invert the order in month 1.
