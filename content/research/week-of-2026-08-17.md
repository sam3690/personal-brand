dated coverage found above — worth modeling the structure, not copying any line:

1. **The vendor-count gut-punch.** "Of the thousands of companies now selling 'AI agents,' only
   ~130 are the real thing" — a huge denominator collapsed into a tiny, damning numerator. Same
   shape as tribe.md hook-bank #3 ("You know your CPL to one decimal place..."). Model this shape

   it still doesn't do" rather than "don't use GHL." Model the tone for angle 3 — teach the gap,
   don't trash the tool an agency owner already pays for.
