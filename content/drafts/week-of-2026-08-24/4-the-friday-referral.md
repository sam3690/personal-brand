The best lead an agency gets all week is usually a referral.
It's also usually the one that sits the longest before anyone replies.

Every agency owner I've talked to has some version of the same story. The referral lands Friday at 4:50pm, right as everyone's closing their laptop. Nobody decides to ignore it. It's just Friday.

It gets answered Monday at 10am. By then two other agencies already called back.

That's not a lead-quality problem, it was the best lead of the week. It's not a team problem either, nobody dropped the ball on purpose. It's a timing problem, and timing problems don't fix themselves with a reminder to check Slack over the weekend.

The data on this isn't close. Leads contacted in the first 5 minutes are roughly 21x more likely to get qualified than ones contacted after 30 (a well-cited MIT/InsideSales study), and that gap only gets wider by Monday.

This is the actual thing I build. Not a chatbot, not a dashboard. A system that replies the second the message arrives, on whatever channel it arrived on, so the answer doesn't depend on who's awake or what day it is.

The plain version: if a referral can sit unanswered from Friday to Monday, that's not a discipline problem, it's a system that only works when someone's watching it.
