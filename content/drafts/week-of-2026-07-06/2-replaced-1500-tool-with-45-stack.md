I replaced a $1,500/mo AI SDR tool with $45/mo of self-hosted n8n. Same job, same architecture, 97% less cost.

Here's the actual stack, no marketing gloss:

- n8n self-hosted on a $5/mo Hetzner VPS: orchestrates everything, unlimited runs
- Claude or GPT API, about $20/mo: drafts personalized outreach, classifies replies
- 3 warmed mailboxes on Instantly, about $15/mo: sends without burning your main domain
- Apollo or Clay, $0-10/mo: pulls and enriches the lead list

Total: $45-50/mo. Artisan and 11x run $1,500-2,000/mo for the same job.

Speaking of Artisan: LinkedIn banned them in January over how their data vendor sourced leads, then reinstated them two weeks later. That's the risk you take when your outreach engine belongs to someone else's roadmap and someone else's compliance department.

The pipeline: ingest leads, enrich with company context, draft with the LLM, send through a warmed inbox, classify the reply as interested, objection, or not now, hand hot leads to a human to close.

The catch, because I'd rather tell you than let you find out: the model is just the engine. A generic LLM dropped straight into sales scores badly on its own. The $45 is the floor. The build quality (the scoring logic, the reply classifier, the retry handling) is what actually makes it work.

This is the same build I run for clients. I did it for myself first.
