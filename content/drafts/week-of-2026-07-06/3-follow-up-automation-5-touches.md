80% of deals need 5+ follow-ups. Most reps give up after 2.

Not because they're lazy. Because manually tracking who's due for touch #3 across 40 open leads is a nightmare nobody has time for.

So I built the automation that doesn't quit:

1. Lead comes in, gets tagged with a next-touch date
2. n8n checks the CRM daily for anyone due
3. Reply detected, anytime? Sequence stops, a human takes over
4. No reply after touch 3? The angle changes (value-add, not "just checking in")
5. No reply after touch 5? Moves to a slow-nurture list, not deleted

One client's pipeline, a representative month: leads that used to get 1-2 touches and go cold were suddenly getting the full 5-touch sequence, every time, without anyone remembering to do it.

The workflow itself is 9 nodes. The hard part wasn't the automation. It was deciding what "due for touch #3" actually means for their sales cycle.
