A fragrance ecommerce brand's WhatsApp used to go quiet for hours.
Now every message gets answered instantly, and sales have climbed on every new launch since.

Before this, the brand ran everything by hand. Social posts scheduled whenever there was time. Ads managed on gut feel. Customer messages on WhatsApp answered whenever someone got a free minute, sometimes hours later, sometimes the next day.

For a brand that lives on new-launch hype, "the next day" is the sale, gone. Someone messaging about a new scent at 9pm wants an answer at 9pm, not an apology the next morning.

I built two systems that talk to each other. A content and ad pipeline in n8n that keeps posts and campaigns running on schedule instead of on memory. And a WhatsApp assistant wired into it that answers every customer message the moment it lands, day or night.

Plain version: nothing about how the brand sells changed. What changed is nobody who messages them waits anymore.

Since it went live: the assistant replies instantly, the content and ad pipeline is driving more engagement on every post, and sales have increased, especially around new launches, exactly the moment a slow reply used to cost them the most.

None of this is complicated technology. It's unglamorous plumbing that stops a customer from deciding you don't care because you took six hours to answer.

If you sell physical products online, what's the actual gap right now between a DM landing and someone on your team seeing it?
