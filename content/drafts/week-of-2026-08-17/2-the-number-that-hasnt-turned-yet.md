Six weeks into the cold email experiment.
Forty sent. Zero replies.

Every morning I open the inbox before anything else. Coffee, laptop, refresh. Looking for the one reply that tells me the niche is real.

Most mornings there is nothing. Some mornings there is nothing twice.

I keep sending anyway, because the alternative is admitting I picked wrong again, and I'm not ready to sit with that yet.

[SPECIFIC INCIDENT — Usama to supply: what happened, what it would have cost, what he changed after. Do not publish until filled in. Keep it about the work only: could not sell, no proof, wrong niche, a mistake in the business itself, fear, money in the business — never a client, never private/family life.]

Zero replies is not proof the offer is dead. It might just be proof I haven't sent enough of them yet. I don't actually know which one is true, and that's the part nobody says out loud: most of this work gets done without knowing, not after knowing.

I'm not writing this because it resolved into something. It hasn't. I'm writing it because six weeks in is a real place to be, and pretending to already be past it would be the more comfortable lie.

What's the number you keep checking every morning before anything else, the one that hasn't turned yet?
