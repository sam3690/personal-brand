GoHighLevel's missed call text back covers one channel.
The DM, the web form, and the WhatsApp message sitting next to it get nothing.
Here's the exact gap, and five moves that close it today.

GoHighLevel's missed call text back is a genuinely good feature. If someone calls and you miss it, it auto-texts them back in seconds so they know you're not being ignored. A lot of you already pay for it and it already works.

Here's what it doesn't do. It only fires on a missed phone call. The same lead who messages your Instagram instead, or fills out your website form, or texts your WhatsApp number, gets nothing. Same business, same urgency, different channel, total silence.

Five moves that close that gap, today, inside the tool you already have:

1. Turn the native trigger on for every number. Settings > Phone Numbers > Missed Call Text Back. Free, and most accounts still have it off on at least one line.

2. Build the same reflex for every other channel. Add a Workflow trigger for the Facebook/Instagram DM, the website chat widget, and every form submission. Route all of them into the same pipeline stage as the phone trigger, not four separate ones.

3. Replace the generic "sorry we missed you" with two real questions. A Send SMS step, then a Wait for Reply step, asking what they need and when they need it. You're not building a bot that sounds human. You're building one that asks the two things you'd ask yourself.

4. Write every channel back to the same contact record. One field for lead source, one for the qualifying answer, one for timestamp, updated no matter which channel triggered it. This is the step that actually closes the gap. Without it, a lead who calls, then DMs, then fills a form shows up as three different people in your pipeline, and three different people get three different follow-ups, or none.

5. Add a workflow that watches the other workflows. A daily check that flags if any channel has gone quiet for 24 hours, so you find out a form broke from a dashboard, not from a lead who complained about you on Google.

None of this is complicated, and none of it costs more than what you already pay for. It's making sure every door into your business gets answered the same way the phone already does.

Which channel are you assuming is covered right now, phone, DM, form, or WhatsApp, that you haven't actually gone and checked?
