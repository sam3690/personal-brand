A medical-sales manager's enquiries used to sit for hours, sometimes a full day.
A voice agent picked up the slack. 14 new customers in three weeks, from leads it caught.

Before this, he was the only one positioned to answer. Every enquiry landed while he was mid-pitch on another call, and by the time he got to it, some of those people had already called someone else.

I built a voice calling agent that answers every inbound enquiry the second it comes in. It asks the same handful of questions he'd ask himself: what they need, their timeline, how to reach them, and logs it straight into his pipeline. The FAQs it's heard a hundred times, it handles alone. Anything past that, it hands back to him.

14 new customers in the last three weeks, from leads the agent caught while he was busy closing something else.

It has no clever moves. It answers the same way every time, and it's never in a meeting. In sales, always available beats occasionally brilliant.

Plain version: whatever you sell, the part that matters is simple. Nobody who reaches out gets ignored because you were doing something else when they did.

What's the last enquiry you lost, not because you said the wrong thing, but because you said nothing in time?
