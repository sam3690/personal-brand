An interior design studio was paying for Meta ads and answering the leads about a day later.

Not out of laziness. He runs the studio on his own. When he is standing in someone's half-finished kitchen, the phone is not in his hand.

Consultation bookings: 7 or 8 a month.

Here is what I changed, and it was not the ads.

Every lead from Meta now lands in an n8n workflow instead of a notification he sees at 9pm. Inside seconds it sends a WhatsApp reply in his voice, not a chatbot voice, because he wrote the lines himself and approved every one of them before it went live. Nothing improvises in front of somebody else's customer.

The reply asks three things. What room. What timeline. Roughly what budget range.

Their answers come back into the workflow. Anyone who clears the bar gets a calendar link in the same thread, while they are still holding the phone. Anyone who does not still gets a real answer, so nobody is left on read.

He wakes up to booked consultations instead of a list of people to chase.

Reply time went from about 24 hours to seconds.
Consultations went from 7 or 8 a month to 27.

His words: "big difference, saving me so much time."

The ads were never the problem. They were doing their job the entire time. The leak was the hours between someone raising their hand and anyone answering, and that gap shows up on no dashboard anywhere, because nothing errors when a lead simply sits there.

Almost nobody is losing deals because their creative is bad.

They are losing them to a Tuesday afternoon.

What is the longest a real enquiry has ever sat in your inbox before somebody got to it?

**FIRST COMMENT:** The question I get every time: "what stops it saying something stupid to my customer?" Nothing generates live. He wrote the messages, he approved them, and the system only ever sends what is already signed off. The judgment stays with the human. The speed is the only part that gets automated.
