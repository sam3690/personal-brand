## THE POST

A broken workflow at my last job didn't lose money.

It lost a patient's records.

I spent 4 years building backend systems for healthcare organizations and agencies. In healthcare, a workflow that drops data doesn't just cost a sale — it loses a record someone's care depends on.

That taught me a rule I've never let go of: automation only counts if it's reliable enough to bet your business on.

So when I started building AI sales agents and CRM systems for founders, I didn't bring "move fast and break things."

I brought "move fast and don't break things."

Most AI demos look incredible for five minutes and fall apart the first time real traffic hits. A node silently drops data. A retry never fires. The lead is gone and nobody notices.

I've shipped a fix to Axios — the HTTP library running in millions of Node apps. I care about systems that hold under load, not ones that win a demo.

That's the whole difference between a chatbot that sounds smart and a system that quietly does the work at 2am.

If you've had an automation break in production — what broke, and what did it cost you?

I post the real builds here, bugs included. Follow along if that's useful.
