## THE POST

If your sales team spends 6 hours a day on lead research, this one's for you.

Most of those 6 hours is work AI should own: research, enrichment, scoring, routing.

I call the fix the Prompt → Pipeline framework. Five steps:

1. Map your current lead flow — every step from "lead arrives" to "call booked."
2. Tag which steps AI can own vs. which still need a human.
3. Build the qualify → score → route logic (every lead gets a score *and a reason* — never a bare number).
4. Add safeguards — retries, a human gate for low-confidence calls — so it doesn't break in production.
5. Measure the real numbers.

When it's running, the numbers move fast: response time from 3 days to under 60 seconds, cost per lead from ~$180 to ~$65, 3x more qualified leads a month.

It's not a magic prompt. It's a system you can build in 6–8 weeks.
