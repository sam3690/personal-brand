Every email my AI SDR drafts has to pass 5 checks before it can touch a real lead. Miss one and the workflow pauses itself instead of sending.

When I swapped Claude Sonnet 5 into the stack last week, the swap took an afternoon. Deciding whether the output was still safe to send took longer. That's the part nobody posts about.

Here's the spot-check rubric I run on sampled drafts every week:

1. Personalization test. Could this exact email go to any other lead unchanged? If yes, the enrichment step failed, not the model.

2. Claim test. Does it promise pricing, features, or timelines nobody approved? A hallucinated promise is the most expensive failure mode an AI SDR has.

3. Classification audit. Pull 10 replies the model tagged "not interested" and reread them. One misclassified interested reply is silent lost revenue.

4. Tone drift. Put the draft next to 3 approved past emails. After any model or prompt change, drift shows up here first.

5. Stop-condition test. Reply to a test sequence from a dummy inbox and watch whether every remaining touch cancels. If one channel keeps firing, nothing else on this list matters.

The model swap cut my LLM line item roughly in half. The rubric is what made the swap safe to do in an afternoon.

What's in your review loop before AI output reaches a real lead? Genuinely curious what others check that I don't.
