The largest benchmark ever run on AI sales agents found that most of them are actively losing you money. Not underperforming. Losing.

Blackpearl's GTM-Bench tested 7 systems across 72 sales tasks against 59,881 real prospecting queries. Scoring was blunt: plus one for a good lead, minus one for a bad one.

4 of 6 general AI agents scored negative overall. Their bad decisions outweighed anything useful they produced.

The winning system scored plus 26,615. GPT-5.5 with proprietary data alone scored plus 4,040. Strip the proprietary data and it drops to plus 1,015.

Read that gap twice. Data alone gave a 4x improvement. Purpose-built architecture on top of that same data gave 26x.

That's the part most agencies selling "AI sales agents" don't want you to sit with: the model was never the moat. You can drop the best LLM on the market into a bad workflow and it will confidently make bad decisions at scale, just faster than a human would have.

The moat is boring: how leads get scored, how replies get classified, how enrichment feeds the decision, what happens when the model is wrong. I've built 500+ of these workflows, and the ones that work are never the ones with the fanciest model. They're the ones where someone thought hard about the 10 ways the decision logic breaks.

If your AI sales agent is just a good model wrapped in nothing, you're paying for the 96%, not the 4%.

Has anyone here actually benchmarked their AI agent against real outcomes, not just demo output? What did you find?
