80% of deals need 5+ follow-ups to close. Most reps stop after 1 or 2, then wonder why the pipeline feels empty.

Nobody quits because they don't care. They quit because tracking who's owed touch 4 on which channel is a full-time job on its own.

Here's the n8n build that follows up for you, exactly 5 times, and knows the instant to stop:

1. Every lead gets a follow-up schedule the moment they enter the pipeline.
Day 0, day 2, day 4, day 7, day 12. Fixed cadence, no one forgotten because someone got busy.

2. Each touch pulls context from the last interaction, not a generic template.
Touch 3 references what they clicked or replied to in touch 1. Feels like a person paying attention, because the workflow is.

3. Every touch checks one thing first: did they already reply anywhere?
Email, LinkedIn, a missed call, doesn't matter which channel. One reply anywhere cancels every future touch instantly.

4. The stop-condition is the whole point.
[YOUR NUMBER, e.g., "before this, I had leads getting a 6th touch after they'd already booked a call"]. That's the moment a prospect stops trusting the system behind your brand.

5. Dead leads get a real exit, not silence.
After touch 5 with zero response, they move to a slow nurture list instead of just disappearing. Some of my best late closes came from month-3 nurture, not the 5-touch sprint.

The follow-up isn't the hard part. Knowing exactly when to stop is.

Where does your pipeline actually die: not enough touches, or too many after someone already said yes?
