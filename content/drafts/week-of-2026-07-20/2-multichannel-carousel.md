**INTRO POST (text above the carousel):**

Multi-channel outbound converts at more than double the rate of single-channel. Most reps still run one channel and call the other two an afterthought.

Here's the automation that runs email, LinkedIn, and phone as one coordinated sequence, and cancels itself the moment someone replies on any of the three:

**CAROUSEL SLIDES:**

1. **Cover:** "Single-channel outbound converts at less than half the rate of a 3-channel sequence. Here's the automation that runs all three."
2. **The problem:** Reps treat email as the sequence and LinkedIn/phone as manual afterthoughts. Timing and messaging don't match across channels, so the lead sees three disconnected touches instead of one coordinated one.
3. **Step 1:** New lead lands in n8n, gets enriched (Clay/Apollo) before anything sends.
4. **Step 2:** Day 0: personalized cold email from a warmed inbox.
5. **Step 3:** Day 2: if the email was opened but not replied to, a LinkedIn connection note fires automatically, referencing the email.
6. **Step 4:** Day 4: still no reply anywhere, a call task auto-creates in the CRM with the lead's context pre-filled, and the rep gets a Slack ping.
7. **Step 5 (the stop condition):** A reply on ANY channel cancels the rest of the sequence instantly and routes the lead to a human. Nobody gets double-touched after they've already responded.
8. **The result:** multi-channel sequences convert 2.3x single-channel, with a 30-40% lift in booking rate when timing and channel choice are optimized (sourced, see post caption).
9. **Save this / close:** "Save this if you're still running one channel. What's the channel you always forget to follow up on?"
