Your hottest lead this week and someone who downloaded a PDF for fun land in the exact same queue, in the exact same order they arrived.

That's not a prioritization problem. It's the absence of one.

Here's the 4-step build that scores and routes leads the second they land, so your best ones never wait:

1. Score on signal, not just source.
Company size, role, what page they were on, whether they used a phrase like "pricing" or "book a call." A lead ad click and a pricing-page form fill are not the same lead.

2. Set 2 thresholds, not 10.
Hot (routes to a human immediately) and everything else (goes to nurture). Overengineering the scoring model is how these projects die in a spreadsheet instead of shipping.

3. Hot leads skip every queue.
Straight to a rep's phone or Slack, with the scoring reasons attached, so they know why this one's hot before they even open the CRM.

4. Everyone else gets the automations from posts #1-4 of this series, not silence.
Instant response, real follow-up, real booking, a CRM that logs it. Nurture isn't a consolation prize, it's a different automation for a different intent level.
