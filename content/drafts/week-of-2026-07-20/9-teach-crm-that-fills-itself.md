Your CRM isn't wrong because your team is careless. It's wrong because updating it after every call is the first thing that gets skipped when the day gets busy.

A CRM nobody trusts is worse than no CRM. You start making decisions off a system that's quietly lying to you.

Here's the 3-step build that keeps it honest without anyone typing a thing:

1. Every call gets recorded and transcribed automatically.
Doesn't matter if it's Zoom, a phone line, or WhatsApp voice notes. The raw transcript is the source of truth, not someone's memory an hour later.

2. Claude reads the transcript and extracts what actually matters.
Deal stage, objections raised, next step, a one-line summary. Not a wall of text nobody will read, the 4 fields your pipeline reports actually run on.

3. The CRM record updates the moment the call ends.
No queue, no "I'll do it tonight." By the time the rep is back at their desk, the record already reflects what just happened.

The unlock isn't the transcription. It's deciding, once, exactly which 4 fields matter, so the automation has one clear job instead of guessing what to log.
