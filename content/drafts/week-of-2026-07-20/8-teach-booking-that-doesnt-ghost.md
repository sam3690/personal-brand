Every double-booked slot and every no-show is a paying customer you already had, and lost anyway.

It's the quietest leak in a service business, because nobody tracks "calls that should have happened."

Here's the build that closes it, 4 steps, runs itself:

1. One calendar link, one source of truth.
No more "does Tuesday work" back-and-forth across email and DMs. The booking link is the only door in.

2. Confirmation fires the second they book, with the actual context of why they're calling.
Not a generic "see you then." A one-line recap of what they asked about, so the call starts warm.

3. Reminders go out on a real cadence: 24 hours, then 1 hour before.
Each one lets them reschedule in one click instead of just not showing up. A reschedule is a saved slot. A no-show is a dead one.

4. A no-show triggers an immediate, honest follow-up, not silence or a guilt trip.
"Looks like we missed each other, here's my link if you want to grab another time." [YOUR NUMBER]% of my no-shows rebook within 48 hours when this fires same-day instead of days later.

Nothing here is clever. It's just a system that remembers to do the boring thing every single time, which is more than most calendars manage on their own.

What's actually costing you more right now: no-shows, or double-books?
