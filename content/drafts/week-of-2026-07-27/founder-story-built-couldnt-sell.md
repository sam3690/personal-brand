# Founder story: "That is not a success story. It is a receipt."

**v2.** v1 was rejected by Usama: it read as a client case study, not his story. The interior-studio
result and its metrics are cut entirely. This is Level 1 of the framework (pure hero), no guide layer,
no proof-by-client. The proof here is emotional honesty and specificity about his own life.

Angle: he is in the messy middle and says so, on purpose. The post deliberately does not resolve
into a win, and gives that non-ending a reason ("almost nobody talks from here").

## Post

I still work 9 to 6.
Nothing I have built has changed that yet.

That is the honest version of my story, and I am going to keep telling it that way.

I am a developer in Karachi. I started building AI automation systems after work. Evenings, weekends, the hours other people use to rest.

Somewhere along the way it stopped being a hobby I could quietly abandon.
I was getting better at building and no closer to owning anything.
The fear got specific: five more years of that, and still nothing of my own at the end.

So I built harder. 500+ workflows in n8n. Systems that actually worked.
Almost nobody bought them.

I would finish the day job, open my inbox, and find the same thing every time. No replies. No serious conversations. Months of it.

I had a salary, so I was never desperate. I was just going nowhere.

What I got wrong took me too long to see.
I was in love with building. I assumed that if the system was good enough, selling would take care of itself.
It does not work that way. It never has.

Things only moved when I stopped describing what I could build and started asking people what their problem was actually costing them.

Where I am today: over $850 earned, a few small clients, no predictable pipeline. Still 9 to 6. Still building at night.

That is not a success story. It is a receipt.

I am posting it because most of us are somewhere in this middle part, and almost nobody talks from here. We only hear the story after it worked, once it has been tidied up.

What is the part of your journey you have not posted about yet?
