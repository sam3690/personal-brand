# Founder story 2: "100 impressions on my best work. 1,500 on the post where I admitted I am not there yet."

**Sequel to `founder-story-built-couldnt-sell.md`** (posted Tue 2026-07-28), which broke every reach
record on the account: 1,500 impressions, 1,056 members reached, 18 reactions, 5 comments, 12 profile
viewers, 3 followers. Prior best in the performance log was 196 impressions.

Angle: the gap between those two numbers IS the insight. The chapter-two move is to report the result
of the honest post honestly, then find the same mistake in his cold email, and name the belief it left
him with. Gives the readers who engaged with chapter one a real payoff instead of a repeat.

Level 1 (hero) for the first two thirds, then a Level 3 turn: names the hole in the "build authority by
teaching value" advice everyone here follows. No guide layer, no client, no proof-by-case-study.

## Post

100 impressions on my best work.
1,500 on the post where I admitted I am not there yet.

Three months of posting here. Frameworks, cost breakdowns, tool stacks, the things I am genuinely good at. Most of them reached about 100 people.

Last week I wrote the honest version instead. That I still work 9 to 6. That I have built 500+ workflows and earned $850. That I am not where I told myself I would be by now.

1,056 people reached. 18 reactions. 5 comments. 12 profile clicks and 3 new followers off one post.

15x the reach for the post that took the least expertise and the most honesty.

Here is the part that actually bothered me.

I went back and read the first 16 cold emails I sent this month. Zero replies. Every one of them was written exactly like my good posts. Clean, competent, entirely about what I can build.

Not one line in any of them that a person could see themselves in.

I had been making the same mistake everywhere. Leading with the finished version of me. The version with no bad months in it.

That version is the hardest one to relate to. It is also the one that is not true yet.

So this is what I am changing, in posts and in emails and on calls: the real number goes before the impressive one. Where I actually am goes before what I can do.

It is Sunday, so I will leave it there.

What is the true sentence about your work that you keep editing out?
