18 days into this business, I almost sold to everyone.

Agencies. Dentists. Med-spas. Interior designers. Whoever would take a call.

Then I ran one test on all three and killed two.

Question 1: does a lost lead actually hurt?
A missed enquiry on a $10k-100k renovation costs real money.
A missed enquiry on a $150 cleaning barely registers.

Question 2: is the pain felt fast?
A design studio feels a slow reply within days.
An agency buries the same problem for a whole quarter.

Question 3: do buyers hand me more buyers?
Designers know designers, contractors, cabinet makers, landscapers.
One happy client turns into three warm introductions.
Dentists and med-spas don't refer sideways like that.

Interior design and home-design studios won on all three.
Agencies won on none: harder to prove, easier to shrug off.
Low-ticket local won one: fast pain, too small a deal.

I spent 4 years building backend systems for healthcare orgs.
There, a broken workflow lost a patient record, not a Tuesday.
I'm bringing that same standard somewhere it's finally worth it.
A dropped enquiry at a design studio isn't an inconvenience.
It's a $20k-100k project walking out the door.
That's the bet: answer every enquiry before it goes cold.

So I stopped trying to be everywhere and picked one room.

How did you decide what to niche into?
Or are you still selling to everyone, hoping something sticks?

I'm building this in public here if you want to watch.
