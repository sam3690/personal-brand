78% of customers buy from the first company that responds.

Every design-studio sales blog quotes this stat.

No one can point to the study.

Because it doesn't exist.

I went looking. The number traces back to nothing.

Every citation loops back to the same aggregator blogs, quoting each other. No original report. No named researcher. No methodology, anywhere.

Here's the speed-to-lead stat that IS real.

The Oldroyd / InsideSales 2007 study, Lead Response Management, found:

→ Contact odds 100x higher when a web lead is worked within 5 minutes instead of 30 minutes.

→ Qualification odds 21x higher over that same window.

Named researcher. Named study. A real methodology behind both numbers.

Now the modern number, and it's worse news.

A 2026 Blazeo benchmark, 573 businesses, found 74% miss the 5-minute window entirely.

Translate that for a design studio:

You are not losing the $40k kitchen project to a better designer.

You are losing it to whoever answered the DM first.

The homeowner didn't compare portfolios and pick the strongest one. They messaged three studios at 9pm.

By the time you replied the next afternoon, they'd already booked a consult with whoever answered in minutes.

Speed isn't a nice-to-have layered on top of good design work.

Speed is the sales process now. Design decides who keeps the client after they're already in the door.

What's your studio's actual average reply time?

Not the number you'd like it to be. Pull your last 10 enquiries and time it for real. Tell me what you find.
