$400 for a lead. $20,000 walks if you're slow to answer.

Here's the exact math on one missed WhatsApp enquiry.

Run it on your own studio's numbers. Five steps.

STEP 1: What you paid for the lead.
Marketplace leads (Angi, HomeAdvisor, Houzz-type) run $150 to $600.
Shared with 3 to 5 competing contractors, not just you.

STEP 2: What the job is actually worth.
Median renovation project value sits at $20,000 (Houzz 2026 study).
Your average ticket is probably different. Use [YOUR NUMBER] instead.

STEP 3: What speed does to your odds.
Reply in 60 seconds instead of a few hours later.
My close-rate delta from doing that: [YOUR NUMBER]. Now find yours.

STEP 4: The no-shows nobody accounts for.
Unverified, volume-driven consult bookings no-show 20 to 40% of the time.
A confirmation text and a reminder close most of that gap.

STEP 5: Add it up.
Lead cost, plus project value times your close-rate delta.
Then subtract whatever the no-show tax takes back.
That's what one ignored WhatsApp message actually costs your studio.

For most studios, that number beats this month's entire ad budget.

Plain version, no jargon: the fix answers every enquiry in under 60 seconds.
It asks the budget and timeline questions, books the consult, texts the reminder.
Nothing clever in it.
It just replies before the lead goes to whoever answered first.

I can't fill in your two numbers. Only you have them.

Run the five steps with your real figures.

Tell me what you got.
I'll tell you if that's normal, or if you're bleeding worse than most.
