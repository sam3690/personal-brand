ZoomInfo just published a benchmark that proves the model was never the moat.

GTM Bench, July 2026: real go-to-market tasks (lead research, account mapping, outreach drafting) scored against ground truth.

ZoomInfo's own GTM.AI: Bench Index 77, 478 verifiable records per 1,000 attempts, $0.79 a task.
Apollo: 47. Exa: 36. Open web search: 31, all at higher cost.

Roughly the same models underneath all four. The gap is entirely the data layer: whose records are actually current.

The AI SDR pitch has always been "the agent is smart." The real moat was never the model. It's whether the thing feeding it actually knows who's still at the company it says they're at.

I'd rather ship a boring agent on clean data than a clever one guessing off six-month-old records.
