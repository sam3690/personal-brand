About a month ago I picked a niche I have never sold into.

Interior design studios. High-ticket rooms, ten to a hundred thousand dollars a project. I have spent four years automating backend systems for healthcare orgs and agencies. Not one of those hours was spent anywhere near a kitchen renovation.

I built the case for it on paper. Deals big enough that a slow reply actually costs something. Referral-dense, so one happy client turns into three more introductions. I still believe the paper case.

Then I tested it against actual studios.

As of my last real count: sixteen contacted, zero replies.

Not one "not interested." Not one "who is this." Nothing.

I run the same three explanations on a loop. The niche is wrong. The offer is wrong. Or sixteen messages just isn't enough data to know anything yet, and I'm impatient for a verdict I don't get to have this early.

I don't know which one it is. I built the system, wrote the guarantee, priced it fair, and I'm sitting here with a list of contacted names and nothing next to most of them.

Everyone who's ever picked a niche talks about it after they know it worked. I'm writing this from the part where I still don't.

What's the bet you're in the middle of right now, the one you can't tell yet if it's smart or just stubborn?
