74% of residential contractors say AI is key to their business. Only 25% actually use it in any meaningful way. Nearly half don't trust it. (ServiceTitan, 2026 State of AI in the Trades report.)

That's not a knowledge problem. Everyone's read the same articles about response times and lost leads. It's an implementation problem. Nobody handed them a system, just more software to configure themselves.

I built one for an interior design studio a few months ago. Anonymized here since we haven't cleared naming, but the shape of it:

Before: every WhatsApp and Instagram enquiry sat until someone had a free minute, sometimes 24 hours later. 7 to 8 consults booked a month.

After: an automation answers within seconds, asks the three questions that actually qualify a $10k-100k project (budget, scope, timeline), books straight into the calendar. 27 consults a month. Same lead volume, same ad spend, roughly 3.5x the booked calls.

Nothing in that system is exotic. A WhatsApp webhook, an n8n workflow, three qualifying questions, a calendar API. The 74% who believe AI matters are right. The 25% who've actually shipped something are the ones seeing it show up in their calendar.

The gap between believing and doing is never really a tools problem. "Automate my lead response" sounds like a project. Most people don't have a free month to build one, so it stays a belief instead of a system.

If you run a business where one missed enquiry is worth five figures: what happens to the message that comes in after everyone's gone home for the day?
