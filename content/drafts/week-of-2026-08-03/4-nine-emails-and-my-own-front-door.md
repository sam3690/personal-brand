I sent nine emails to nine design studios in Austin on Monday.
I followed up on Thursday.
Nothing came back.

So I opened my own LinkedIn profile and read it the way one of them would have.

The headline said: GTM Agents + AI automation for Founders & agency owners.

When I sent those emails I did not know what a resale certificate was. I did not know which vendors ask for it before they will quote you net. I did not know that "the acknowledgment says 22 weeks" is a sentence that wrecks a date somebody already promised a client out loud.

I have spent the eleven days since finding out.

The emails went out before any of that. The subject line said their leads were going cold. Under it, a price and a deadline and a request for fifteen minutes, from someone none of them had ever heard of. All nine pointed at that headline.

If any one of the nine checked me at all, she checked once, on her phone, somewhere between a jobsite and the slab yard. And the first line she read was written for somebody else.

I changed the headline this week. It now says I answer the inquiries you miss while you are on a jobsite.

The number is still zero.

The honest version is that the sign on the door was wrong and the letter was wrong too, and I fixed the cheaper one first. I will not know for weeks whether the sign was ever the reason.
