Two half-built workflows in your account, both last saved over a month ago.

The reason neither is finished is not time. You picked the interesting one.

Here is the rule I use instead.

Open either one and it is the same picture. A Webhook node, an OpenAI node, nothing connected after them.

That is not a skill gap. Nobody handed you a way to tell the expensive problem from the interesting one, so you picked the interesting one. Everyone does.

Ten minutes and a notebook. Nothing open in n8n or Make.

Your first build was a content agent or a research agent. That is not carelessness. Content and research are visible. They look like work. You can watch them happen.

The wait between a lead arriving and a human answering it does not look like work. It looks like nothing. Nobody is doing anything during it.

That is exactly why it never made the list.

The first wait I ever put a number on was at a medical-sales team: inbound inquiries sitting for hours before anyone answered, some of them all day. Nobody had ever called that a task, which is why nobody had ever automated it. The system that closed that gap booked 14 new customers in three weeks from leads it caught.

And the only build instruction in there: ship the top line ugly, at six nodes, with an Error Workflow pointed at it before its first real run. Not after. Six nodes on its own is a demo. Six nodes with something watching it is the smallest real thing you can own.

Know what that buys you and what it does not. An Error Workflow only fires on a run that throws. A run that quietly does nothing never reaches it. That is the whole reason slide five says pick the one that fails in front of you first.

Automate the gap where something is waiting on a person, not the task the person is doing.

What is the longest wait in your business right now, in hours? Not the biggest task. The longest wait.

And if you want the order rather than the number: if none of your list has numbers next to it, send it anyway and I will tell you which three to go get. If it does have numbers, send me the top three and I will tell you which one I would build first and why.

**FIRST COMMENT:** Slide 3 asks for three numbers and most lists have zero. If you only get one, get how long it sits. Frequency and value you can estimate. The wait is the one people are always wrong about, and always in the same direction.
