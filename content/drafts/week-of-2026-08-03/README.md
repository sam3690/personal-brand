# Week of 2026-08-03 — the mix, and why

Rewritten 2026-08-03 after two rounds of CEO feedback. Two corrections drove this version.

## Correction 1: hard character ceiling

LinkedIn's limit is **3,000 characters**, not words. The previous drafts ran 2,139 to 3,286 and two of
them were over the ceiling outright. More importantly, length was never the target: the account's only
breakout post (2026-07-28) was **1,690 characters**. This week runs 1,391 to 1,730. Nothing is padded
to fill a budget.

## Correction 2: the founder-story register is back, and teaching posts were missing

The previous version retired the founder-story register entirely, reading the CEO's "do not be too
honest about failure" note as a ban on the whole format. That was too broad. The standing rules already
written into `../week-of-2026-07-27/founder-story-02-honest-version-outperformed.md` say what the ban
actually covers:

- **Never disclose method.** Do not publish what Usama will do differently in his own sales or content.
  The reader gets the insight, nobody gets the playbook.
- **Never show commercial weakness.** No cash position, no client count, no "nobody bought it".
- **Never rank his own posts against each other.** Contrast the category, never the quality.
- **No private life.** Work only.
- **Land the turn on people, not on him.** The universal observation is the shareable part.

Post 1 is written to all five. It is the register that produced 1,500 impressions at 95% out-of-network
against a ~100-impression baseline, and it is the single most valuable asset this account has.

**Teaching posts were missing and are now half the week.** `../../knowledge-base/winning-post-patterns.md`
already flagged the Teach format as "a first-class P3 format, currently underused." Posts 2 and 4 are
the two highest-save teach structures: the Playbook (N steps, do-it-today) and the Mistakes List. The
account has earned exactly one save in its entire history. That is the gap these close.
