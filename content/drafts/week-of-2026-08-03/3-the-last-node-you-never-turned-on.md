Everything about the account is wired except the one thing that would actually save it: the part where a lead becomes a reply.

You know which client this is. You've known for months.

It isn't that you don't know how to fix it. It's that fixing it means putting something automated in front of your client's customer, and if it says one wrong thing, that isn't a bug report. That's the account.

So here's the actual answer, the one I'd give you on a call, not in a sales page.

You approve every line before it ever sends. It only ever says what you wrote. Nothing improvises in front of someone else's customer.

I can make that promise because of where I learned to build. Four years on backend systems for healthcare orgs, where the same rule wasn't optional: a wrong message doesn't cost you a like, it costs someone their record. That standard doesn't get lowered because the account is smaller.

I built this for one studio. Ads into a WhatsApp booking flow, every reply pre-approved, nothing generated live in front of a stranger. Reply time went from about a day to seconds. Nobody wrote a word by hand, and nobody was surprised by what it said, because they'd already seen it.

The fear was never the build. It was losing control of what got said in your name.

Which client relationship are you most afraid to touch, not because you can't build the fix, but because you can't afford to be wrong in front of them?

**FIRST COMMENT:** The follow-up I always get: "what if my client wants their own tone, not a generic one?" That's the actual work. You write it, or I write it with you, in their voice, and it never sends anything that wasn't already read and signed off. The control stays with the agency. That's the whole point.
