# The Golden Hour

The first 60–90 minutes after publishing decide reach (Phase 2 of [[LinkedIn Algorithm 2026]]).
For a small/cold account this is half the battle.

**Best slots (audience timezone — Usama's ICP skews US/EU):** Tue 7:30–9:00 AM · Wed 8:00–10:00 AM · Thu 7:30–9:00 AM. Avoid Saturday and after 6 PM Friday.

**Ritual every time:**
- Warm the feed: 3–5 thoughtful comments on others' posts *before* publishing.
- Do NOT edit the post for 60 min (early edits penalised).
- Reply to every comment substantively (reply loops = reach). Ask a question back.
- Link/asset → first comment at T+10–15 min, never the body.
- Consistency: 4 posts/week beats 1 perfect/month; a missed week costs 20–40% reach.
