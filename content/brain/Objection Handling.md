# Objection Handling

Six objections a cold US/EU buyer actually raises against the Speed-to-Lead offer, destroyed with the real guarantee and [[Proof Assets]], not generic sales rebuttals: already using another tool, distrust of AI, "prove it works," "$500 seems cheap," "let me think about it," "we tried automation before and it broke."

**The delivery rule, from [[CLOSER Sales Framework]]:** diagnose before pitching. Most of these evaporate if the discovery call already surfaced the real fear before a price was ever quoted.
