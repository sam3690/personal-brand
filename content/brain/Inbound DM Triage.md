# Inbound DM Triage

Strangers who DM asking to "connect" or "collaborate" are mostly peers with no budget. The job
is to find out which in ONE message without burning the relationship. Mirror image of
[[Warm Reconnect ACA]] (you reaching out) and [[Cold Outreach Engine]] (automated, strangers).

**Rule zero, register:** match their message length. One-liners get one or two lines back and
exactly one question. A four-paragraph reply to a one-line message reads as "waiting to be
asked," which signals pitch, not peer. The offer explanation belongs in exchange two or three.

**The filter question:** "Depends what collaborate means on your side, running the outbound or
trading notes?" Someone hunting free labor has no good answer to it. Their reply sorts them into
paid pilot, cheap peer call, or let it die.

**Diagnosis is free, deliverable is paid.** Saying "your ICP is too wide" or "don't send cold
from your brand domain" costs 20 minutes and buys credibility. The filtered list, the written
sequence, the warmed inboxes are the paid work. Never do a "quick audit" as a favor, that is the
trap in every one of these threads. Deliver the diagnosis as questions, not verdicts, or three
stacked criticisms land as an attack and they stop buying. See [[Objection Handling]].

**The proof boomerang:** the moment you tell someone they have no proof, the next question is
what results you've got. Have the honest version ready. Bluffing loses the deal and the peer.
See [[Proof Assets]].
