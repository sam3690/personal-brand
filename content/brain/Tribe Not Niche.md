# Tribe Not Niche

**Clone tribal posts, never billboards.**
- A **niche** is a spreadsheet row ("small business owners"). Nobody lies in bed identifying as one.
- A **tribe** is who someone already is, with an enemy, a language, and objects ("the high earner who
  did everything right and feels punished for it"). A campfire they already sit around.
- A **billboard** = a viral post that spoke to everyone → huge reach, zero clients (MrBeast gets 100M
  views, zero recognition). A tribal post reaches the right 40 people who each feel hand-picked.

**Two tests before cloning anything:**
1. **Recognition** — does the right person go "holy shit, that's me", not "ha, nice"?
2. **Alignment** — is that tribe the same one your offer serves?

Reach was never the goal; recognition + alignment are. Related: [[The Colouring-in Book]] · [[Recognition Markers]]
