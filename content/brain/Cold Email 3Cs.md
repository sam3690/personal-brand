# Cold Email 3 C's

Alex Berman's cold-email structure: Compliment (specific, real), Case Study (proof you've solved this exact problem), Call-to-Action (one clear ask). Usama's touch 1 already had the Case Study and CTA, missing a genuine Compliment, generic flattery reads as spam.

**Free compliment source:** the Apify Google Maps prospect batches capture `rating` and `reviews` for every local-service lead, "sitting at 4.9 stars across 200+ reviews" is true, specific, and costs zero extra research.

**Aaron Ross's lesson (Cold Calling 2.0):** separate prospecting time from relationship/closing time, don't context-switch between cold-blast mode and warm-conversation mode in the same block. Referrals and inbound beat interruption, every [[Warm Reconnect ACA]] that ends in a referral outweighs many cold sends.
