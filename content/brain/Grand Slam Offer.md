# Grand Slam Offer

The Speed-to-Lead offer rebuilt on Hormozi's Value Equation: raise Dream Outcome and Perceived Likelihood, shrink Time Delay and Effort. The single unlock was risk reversal, a stranger with no testimonials asking for $500 needed a guarantee, not just a lower price.

**The guarantee (confirmed):** Tier 1, conditional delivery, "not live and answering in 7 days, you don't pay." Chosen because the promised event is 100% inside Usama's control, bold to the buyer, near-zero real risk to him. Move to a money-back guarantee after the first testimonial; earn the way to a performance guarantee only once [[Proof Assets]] has real numbers.

**Value stack:** ~$6,800 in component value anchored against a $500 price for the first 2 clients. The gap between anchor and price is the "feel stupid saying no" mechanism.
