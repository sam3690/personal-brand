# The Colouring-in Book

Matt Lakajev's core method (article: "How to Book 10 Meetings From 1 LinkedIn Post").
**You're not writing. You're colouring in.** Don't start from a blank page. Take a post that already
*pulled real inbound for a tribe*, pull out its [[The Seven Bones|bones]] (the structure), and refill
them with YOUR colours: your tribe, your object, your words, the [[Find Your Object|10:32pm thought]].

**Why it beats both a template and a blank page:** a template comes out sounding like everyone who
filled the same blanks (your tribe smells it). A blank page makes you do two jobs at once — invent
structure AND fill it. Cloning a proven stencil removes the structure job; you spend all your effort
on the only thing that was ever yours: the colours.

**Proof it travels:** one planner's payslip post → 56 inbound + 10 calls off a <300-connection account.
Same bones re-coloured for a $70M Aussie property CEO → 175,000 impressions. Different tribes, same skeleton.
