# LinkedIn Algorithm 2026

How a post lives or dies, in 4 phases:
1. **Quality Filter (0–5s)** — no external links in body, scannable formatting, the hook must pass fast.
2. **Seed Audience Test (60–90 min)** — shown to 1–5% of your network; measures engagement *velocity*. Where small accounts die. See [[The Golden Hour]].
3. **Expansion** — wins here go to 2nd-degree + interest audiences. Comment quality, reply loops, saves.
4. **Editorial/Viral (top ~5%)** — humans surface it; lifespan 24–48h → 7+ days.

The 5 structural 2026 changes:
- **Dwell time is scored** (360Brew model) — write for read-time, not clicks.
- **Profile–content alignment enforced** → see [[Profile Content Alignment]].
- **AI-generated text detected & suppressed** — specificity is the defence; see [[Recognition Markers]].
- **Hashtags barely matter** — ≤3, theme-of-content sets your topic.
- **Creator Mode gone** — boost comes from consistency + on-topic history.
