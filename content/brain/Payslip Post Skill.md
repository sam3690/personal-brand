# Payslip Post Skill

The installed skill that runs the [[The Colouring-in Book]] method for you.
**Location:** `.claude/skills/the-payslip-post/` · **Trigger:** say "run the payslip post" /
"write me a payslip post" / "do the recognition post for my industry".

**What it does:** interviews you ONE question at a time about your tribe, finds your [[Find Your Object|object]],
then builds a finished post in *your* voice with **no CTA**. Not a fill-in template — it makes you bring the colours.

**Flow:** DECODE (show the [[The Seven Bones|bones]]) → RE-COLOUR (7-question interview) → BUILD → CHECK (honest read).

**The laws it won't break:** one question at a time · the object is non-negotiable · [[Tribe Not Niche|tribe not billboard]] ·
no CTA ever · output is your voice not Matt's · real numbers or none · run the anti-AI pass.

Use this for recognition-led inbound posts; use [[The Five Agents]] (`/linkedin-post`) for the regular
pillar cadence. They share the same brain. Related: [[How These Fit Together]]
