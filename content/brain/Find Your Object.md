# Find Your Object

The hardest, non-negotiable bone (#3 of [[The Seven Bones]]). The **object** is the one artefact your
tribe handles constantly that all their pain lives inside. Point at it; don't describe the feeling —
hold up the thing and let the reader's own brain do the recognising.

Examples of "the payslip" per tribe:
- NHS consultants → the payslip scanned to the bottom-right every month.
- Aussie high earners → Employment Hero payslip + the CommBank "in your pocket" box.
- Agency founders → the **business bank balance the morning after payroll runs**.
- A bookkeeper's tradies → the shoebox of receipts in the ute.

Find it: *what do they open every week and feel their gut drop? What object recurs in the exact moment
the pain spikes?* If you can't name it, the post has no centre of gravity — stop and find it first.
