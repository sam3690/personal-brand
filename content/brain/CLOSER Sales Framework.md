# CLOSER Sales Framework

Hormozi's 6-step sales-call structure (from 4,000+ personal closes): Clarify why they're here, Label their problem, Overview past attempts (the pain cycle), Sell the vacation not the plane ticket, Explain away concerns, Reinforce the decision.

**The core correction:** most sellers spend 80% of a call pitching (S) and 20% diagnosing (C-L-O). Hormozi's data says flip it, the sale is mostly made before the system is ever described.

**Applied:** replaces the old 5-step discovery-call outline, which was mostly O and S with no explicit C (why today) or R (confirm the yes after they say it). [[Objection Handling]] is the E step.
