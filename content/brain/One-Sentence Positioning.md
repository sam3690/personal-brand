# One-Sentence Positioning

Level 3 of [[StoryMarketing Hero Guide]]: how you stop being a helpful expert and become "the guy".

Every market has **holes**: reasons the currently-preached method doesn't work. If there were none,
everyone in the niche would be winning. Find the hole, name it publicly, and you get credited with the
discovery. Paul Graham's version: stay quiet until you find the thing that doesn't work, then say it.

Questions that surface the hole:
- What is the market complaining about?
- What roadblock keeps recurring no matter what people try?
- What are the gurus preaching that does not actually work?

**The formula:**
> "I'm the **[X guy]** who believes **[Y contrarian thing]** because of **[Z experience]**."

The sentence is supposed to split the market. Half self-filter away, half become diehards. If nobody
disagrees with your Y, you haven't found a hole, you've found a platitude.

Working draft for Usama (interior-design niche, see `content/strategy/current-strategy.md`):
"I'm the guy who builds the boring 60-second reply system for design studios, because I've watched a
$40k project get lost to a 24-hour reply, not to a competitor."
