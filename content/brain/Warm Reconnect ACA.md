# Warm Reconnect (A-C-A)

The fastest path to client #1 isn't a cold stranger, it's someone who already knows Usama. Distinct from [[Cold Outreach Engine]] (strangers, automated), this is personal, manual, and low-volume by design.

**A-C-A structure:** Acknowledge the relationship, Compliment something real and current, Ask a low-friction question. Message 1 never pitches, its only job is to earn a reply.

**The pivot that mattered:** the first "warm list" Usama surfaced (UF Designs, Descent Perfumes, a medical-sales GTM manager) turned out to be friends he'd already built for, not paying clients. That changes the ask entirely: no sales pitch to friends, just a testimonial + permission to use the build as a case study, plus a referral ask. See [[Proof Assets]].
