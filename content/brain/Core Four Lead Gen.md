# Core Four Lead Gen

Hormozi's $100M Leads framework: every lead-gen method is Warm/Cold × One-to-One/One-to-Many, only four cells exist. Usama runs 3 of 4: Warm Outreach ([[Warm Reconnect ACA]]), Post Free Content (the LinkedIn/X engines), Cold Outreach ([[Cold Outreach Engine]]). Paid Ads is the fourth, not active, and shouldn't be until the offer is proven.

**More, Better, New:** push volume in a working cell until it hits a real constraint, then optimize, only then expand. Usama is pre-More right now (low volume everywhere), so [[First Client Sprint]]'s daily minimums exist to force that before any script-rewriting or ad-spend detour.

**Engaged Leads:** only a reply, comment, or click counts as a lead. A send is not a lead. This is the same "conversations started" metric [[First Client Sprint]] tracks.
