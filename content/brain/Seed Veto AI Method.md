# Seed Veto AI Method

How "written with AI in seconds" is true without being slop: **"I feel, AI builds, I judge."**
- **The seed (only a human can):** the raw feeling has to come from someone who actually felt it
  (Matt's own first-payslip gut-drop). The AI has never felt it; it can't seed the post.
- **The build (AI, fast):** structure, drafting, ten variations, word-bank, fact-checking — seconds,
  *because* the slow work was done first: a "genome" (~750k words on one person + tribe: how they talk,
  fears, objects, public beliefs vs 10:32pm ones). The AI reads the map; it doesn't invent the world.
- **The veto (only a human can):** AI gives ten versions, nine slightly off. You kill them until one
  makes your body go "yes, that's it." That click is the only quality control that matters.
