# Engagement Signals

Not all engagement is equal. Ranked by 2026 weight:
1. **Comments** (substantive) — 🔥 highest. A post with 50 comments beats one with 500 likes.
2. **Reposts** — extends to new networks.
3. **Saves** — private, high-intent.
4. **Reactions (likes)** — volume helps, *speed in hour 1* matters more.
5. **Profile clicks** — "who wrote this?"
6. **External link clicks** — ⚠️ NEGATIVE. Links go in the first comment, after 10–15 min.

**Design law:** start conversations, don't collect reactions. End posts with ONE specific
question — except a [[The Seven Bones|payslip post]], which deliberately uses [[The Silent Tail|no CTA]].
