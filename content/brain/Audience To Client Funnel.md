# Audience To Client Funnel

The bridge between content and revenue: [[Content Pillars|content]] earns a profile visit → golden-hour comments/DMs are Engaged Leads (see [[Core Four Lead Gen]]) → soft CTA opens a conversation → a discovery call runs [[CLOSER Sales Framework]] → close with the [[Grand Slam Offer]] guarantee → the delivered build becomes a new [[Proof Assets]] entry and a build-in-public post, closing the loop.

**Why it exists:** content is currently optimized for audience growth (the right call for a 9-week-old account), but growth alone doesn't pay. Every inbound DM from a post should be worked like a warm lead, not left on read, a fourth, reactive lane alongside [[First Client Sprint]]'s three daily-minimum lanes.
