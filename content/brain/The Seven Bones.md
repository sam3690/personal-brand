# The Seven Bones

The payslip stencil — seven load-bearing moves. Each survived the re-colour to a new tribe (that's
how you know it's a bone, not a flourish). Planner's colour → property-CEO re-colour:

1. **Logline hook** — exact person + painful number. ("£10k rise → £2,400 in pocket" → "$10k rise → clears ~4 grand")
2. **Now-moment** — news the whole tribe is processing this fortnight. (pay review body → new super tax, 5 days out)
3. **The object** — their "payslip", the artefact pain lives inside. *Hardest, most important.* See [[Find Your Object]].
4. **Deduction stack** — the unsayable truth said in arithmetic, so nobody breaks the taboo out loud.
5. **Locked-away twist** — close the "but it's still mine really" escape hatch. (pension locked till 60)
6. **The 10:32pm thought** — the private thought they never say. (missing the kids' footy game)
7. **No CTA** — deliberate silence out-pulls "DM me". See [[The Silent Tail]].
