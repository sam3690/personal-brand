# Research Scout

Agent 0, the front of the pipeline (runs before the writing team). Each week it scouts what is
winning on LinkedIn right now in the AI-automation niche: 5-10 posts with 100+ engagement (their
hooks, formats, angles) plus 3-5 timely news pegs. It outputs a dated brief to
`content/research/week-of-<date>.md` that the [[The Five Agents|Strategist]] uses to pick the week's
4 angles. Tools: web search, Apify (scrape top creators and Usama's own posts), /last30days. It keeps
the [[Winning Post Patterns]] register fed with fresh, current proof instead of static examples, and
it is wired into the Sunday routine so research drives the weekly schedule.
