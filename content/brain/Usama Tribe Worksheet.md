# Usama Tribe Worksheet

Fill this before writing a payslip post. Candidate answers seeded from [[Usama Brand Profile]] —
replace with what's *true and specific* from real clients. The [[Payslip Post Skill]] will ask these
one at a time.

1. **Tribe (identity, not niche):** _candidate:_ "The agency founder doing real revenue who pays
   everyone — tools, contractors, ads — before themselves, and still does lead follow-up at 11pm."
2. **Common enemy:** _candidate:_ manual follow-up + leads going cold while competitors reply in seconds;
   the "we'll just hire an admin" trap.
3. **Now-moment (this fortnight):** _candidate:_ the wave of "we hit $X ARR / we're scaling with AI" posts;
   a fresh AI-agent hype cycle. → land *inside* it.
4. **Their object (the "payslip") — DO NOT SKIP:** _candidates to test:_ the CRM/pipeline opened Monday
   morning full of leads that never got a reply · the Slack/email ping of a form fill nobody actioned ·
   the bank balance the morning after payroll. _Pick the one their gut drops at._ → [[Find Your Object]]
5. **Unsayable truth (said in maths):** _candidate:_ "I look like I'm scaling, but I lose 3 deals a week
   to slow follow-up and I'm the bottleneck." Express as a deduction stack (leads in → replied → after 1hr → booked).
6. **10:32pm thought:** _candidate:_ "How many deals died today because nobody followed up — and I built
   a company that still needs me awake to answer a lead?"
7. **A real client who looked like the reader:** _your answer:_ ____________ (grounds the post; not named in it).

Then: run the [[Payslip Post Skill]] → check against [[How These Fit Together]] → post via [[The Golden Hour]].
