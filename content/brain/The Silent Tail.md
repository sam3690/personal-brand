# The Silent Tail

Why these posts compound — and why you run them on a **cadence, not once.**
The visible inbound (e.g. 56 requests) is the small part. Only ~5% of any audience is in-market right
now. The other **95% felt the same gut-drop of recognition and did nothing visible** — didn't like,
comment, or connect. They're the real asset.

A tribal post plants something that keeps firing: every time the tribe handles their [[Find Your Object|object]]
(monthly payslip, the post-payroll bank balance), the post replays in their head. They DM in 6, 12, 18
months — the next time their own object lands wrong. So run the stencil **again next week**, fresh paint.
The visible spike is nice; the silent tail is the business. Costs nothing extra — you planted it on day one.
