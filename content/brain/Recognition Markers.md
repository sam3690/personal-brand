# Recognition Markers

Why the object works: **biology, not copywriting.** The right person's body recognises a familiar
object (their own payslip layout, the Employment Hero logo) in under ~200ms — before the thinking
brain catches up. "That's mine." The old craft (clever hooks, AIDA, "5 ways to") is legacy; what
decides landing is whether recognition fires fast enough to feel like truth.

Mechanisms:
- **Shibboleth** — the exact in-group word ("NHS consultants", "DDRB"), a password outsiders don't use. Say "doctors" and you lose them.
- **Object > description** — point at the artefact, don't narrate the emotion.
- **Maths as indirect speech** — the deduction stack says the unsayable thing in numbers; no taboo broken.
- **Honesty rule** — never label the object as something it isn't ("in your pocket today", not "Net Pay"). A lie voids the recognition. This is also the anti-AI defence (algorithm change #3): specificity no model can fake.
