# AI Agency Niche & Productization

2026 market consensus on good AI-agency niches: buyers have money, the problem is repetitive and boring, current solutions are expensive/manual, the outcome fits one sentence. Named niches include construction estimating, legal intake, real-estate lead qualification, medical patient reminders, e-commerce returns.

**Validates Usama's ICP from outside data**, not just instinct: the medical-sales voice-agent build and the local-service segment (interior design, med spas, roofing, see [[Proof Assets]] and the batch-04 prospect list) both land directly in the market's recommended niches.

**The warning:** "build-a-bot-for-anyone" is a broken model. Usama's fixed-scope, one-outcome offer already avoids it, don't loosen scope chasing a bigger deal.

**Next niche move:** only after [[First Client Sprint]]'s kill-criteria checkpoint (1-2 closes), narrow further into whichever segment is actually replying.
