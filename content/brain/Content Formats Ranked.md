# Content Formats Ranked

By 2026 algorithmic weight:
1. 🏆 **Carousel / document** — 48–72h shelf life; each swipe = dwell signal. Frameworks, step-by-steps.
2. 🥈 **Native video** (<90s, captions burned in — 80% watch on mute).
3. 🥉 **Single image** — only if the image carries a *standalone* framework (or a [[Find Your Object|recognition object]]).
4. **Text-only** — can go huge if the hook is irresistible; hook does 100% of the work.
5. ⚠️ **Polls** — downweighted; monthly at most.

Kills reach: links in body · blurry/template carousels · re-sharing news without commentary · >3 hashtags · off-topic · obvious AI text · engagement bait.
