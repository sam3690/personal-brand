# Profile Content Alignment

A 2026 rule (and Usama's #1 historic leak): the algorithm cross-references every post against
your headline, About, and experience. Aligned = broader reach + "higher confidence." Misaligned =
immediate suppression.

**What hurt Usama:** off-topic reposts (Palestine, self-discipline, salary stories) + 5 cert/badge
posts told LinkedIn the account isn't about AI automation → real posts (0–6 reactions) got throttled.

**Fix (do before posting):** add a Featured section, turn on Services, stop the reposts/badges,
keep 100% on [[Content Pillars]]. Full audit: `content/knowledge-base/profile-audit.md`.
