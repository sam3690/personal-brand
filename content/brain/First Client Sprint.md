# First Client Sprint

Everything narrowed to one goal: close 1 paying Speed-to-Lead client in 14 days. Built because the real bottleneck wasn't strategy (the [[Grand Slam Offer|offer]] and [[Cold Outreach Engine|outreach engine]] were already sound), it was volume, ~26 lifetime cold touches and 1-2 LinkedIn accepts.

**Three lanes, daily minimums:** 5 warm reconnects ([[Warm Reconnect ACA]]), 10 new cold emails + due follow-ups, work any LinkedIn accepts (don't send more requests, near the cap).

**The one metric:** conversations started per day, a reply that continues, not a send or an open. Two zero-conversation days in a row means the offer/message is broken, not the volume.

**Do-not-touch this sprint:** no new pillars, no product launches, no price drops below $500, no dropping the guarantee.
