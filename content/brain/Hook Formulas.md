# Hook Formulas

Only ~2–3 lines show before "see more" — the hook is most of the game. Six that work:
1. **Counterintuitive** — "Posting every day is making your LinkedIn worse."
2. **Specific-number promise** — "0→40K in 14 months. Here's the exact system 👇"
3. **Personal confession** — "I made $0 from LinkedIn for 8 months. Then I changed one thing."
4. **Bold claim** — "The algorithm doesn't reward consistency. It rewards conversation."
5. **"If you" targeting** — "If you're a founder posting and getting silence — this is why."
6. **List tease** — "7 posts that each drove 10+ inbound conversations:"

The payslip stencil uses a 7th: the **logline hook** = exact person + painful number
("£10k rise → £2,400 in your pocket"). See [[The Seven Bones]].
Hooks fail when vague, hedged, or they give the payoff away. Related: [[LinkedIn Algorithm 2026]]
