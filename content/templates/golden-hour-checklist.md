# Posting & Golden-Hour Checklist

> Print this. Run it every time you publish. The first 60–90 minutes decide reach (Phase 2).
> A great post posted wrong still dies. This is half the battle for a small account.

## Before you publish
- [ ] Post is QA-PASSED (Agent 5 ≥80, zero red flags).
- [ ] Slot is a **Tier-1 window** in your audience's timezone:
      Tue 7:30–9:00 AM · Wed 8:00–10:00 AM · Thu 7:30–9:00 AM (US/EU founders).
- [ ] ≤3 hashtags (ideally 0). No link in the body.
- [ ] You have a 90-minute window free to reply. If not, reschedule.
- [ ] **Warm the feed:** leave 3–5 thoughtful comments on others' posts in your niche first.

## At publish (T+0)
- [ ] Publish. Do **NOT** edit for 60 min (early edits are penalized).
- [ ] Send the post to 3–5 relevant people / a pod via DM to seed early engagement (real humans only).

## Golden hour (T+0 to T+90 min)
- [ ] Reply to **every** comment with a substantive reply (not "thanks!") — reply loops = reach.
- [ ] Ask a follow-up question back in each reply to extend the thread.
- [ ] If a link/asset is referenced: drop it in the **first comment at T+10–15 min**, not at T+0.
- [ ] Comment on 3–5 more niche posts to keep your activity warm.

## After
- [ ] Log result in `../performance-log.md` (create on first use): date, pillar, format, hook formula,
      likes / comments / reposts / saves at 24h, and the comment-to-like ratio.
- [ ] Note what to repeat. Double down on whatever pillar+format+hook earns the best comment ratio.
